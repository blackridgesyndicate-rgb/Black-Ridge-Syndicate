## Weighting logic (INFERENCE, pending your letter)
- **Safety (25) + Rules/Business (20) = 45 items.** These are the most rule-based sections, and they respond fastest to memorization. They come first.
- **Steep-slope (35)** is the biggest single section. It gets the most calendar time.
- **Low-slope (15) + Non-res (15) = 30** commercial items. Residential roofers usually lose the most points here.
- **Materials/Prep (20)** is woven through the steep-slope and low-slope weeks.

## Daily routine (weekday, 90 min)
1. **15 min:** flashcards (previous days' memorize boxes).
2. **45 min:** new guide section, read actively and redo the worked examples on paper.
3. **20 min:** 10–15 practice items on today's topic (from the mocks after week 4; before that, the "common wrong answers" tables serve as practice).
4. **10 min:** write down 3 things you got wrong or didn't know. Review them tomorrow.

## What changes when you send your letter
If the letter shows section percentages, I will:
- move your two weakest sections to the front and give them about 40% of the remaining hours;
- add extra drill questions (beyond the two mocks) for those sections;
- shorten time on any section where you scored ≥ 75%.
