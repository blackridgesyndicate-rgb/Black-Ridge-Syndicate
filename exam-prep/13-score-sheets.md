# Score Sheets and the 70% Benchmark

**Important:** a mock score is **not** a prediction of your CTS result. These are original practice items, not CTS questions. Use your section scores to find weak areas, not to forecast a pass.

## Passing benchmark
- CTS passing grade: **70** (S1 bulletin). On 130 questions, that means **91 correct**.
- The practice-exam tab on the study page scores you automatically by section.
- To score a paper attempt, use the table below.

## After each mock
1. Record your section scores here, or in the study page's results panel.
2. For every miss, read the explanation **and** why the answer you picked was wrong. Write the trap in one line.
3. Re-read the matching guide section's "What to memorize" and "How to reason" boxes.
4. Send me your section scores. I'll adapt the next round of drills to your weak areas.
