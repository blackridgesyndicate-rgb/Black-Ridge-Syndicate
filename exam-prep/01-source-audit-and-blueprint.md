**Consequence (INFERENCE):** 7 of the 9 named references are paywalled books I cannot open. For their subject matter I rely on the public primary sources that those books themselves teach from:
- **OSHA 29 CFR 1926** (Subparts E, K, L, M, X, Z; §1926.1101 asbestos; §1926.1153 silica). The NRCA safety books are built on these. **VERIFIED (primary)** for §1926.502 fall-protection criteria. Other OSHA citations are from the CFR text as I know it and are marked where you should double-check.
- **NRCA public material** (Professional Roofing articles, NRCA's CERTA torch-safety manual, which is public on NRCA's site).
- **Model codes (IRC/IBC)** for dimensions. **Illinois has no statewide residential building code.** Local jurisdictions adopt codes, so every code dimension is flagged **[JURISDICTION]**.
- **Manufacturer installation instructions** for system-specific numbers. Flagged **[MANUFACTURER]**.

I will **never** write "NRCA says X" as a quotation. Where I attribute a practice to NRCA, I label it "NRCA practice (from public NRCA material / widely published; verify in manual)". I only use that label for well-established points.

### 1.3 Edition-mismatch warning (INFERENCE, but important)
- The CTS guide is dated **May 2021** and lists the **2018–2021** NRCA volumes. Every NRCA volume has since been superseded (2023–2026). The **CTS item bank may still be keyed to the older editions.** On most fundamentals (flashing heights, laps, slopes, asphalt types) the editions agree. Where later editions changed guidance, I flag it **[EDITION]**.
- The **Illinois Act changed on Aug 15, 2025 (P.A. 104-427).** Maximum discipline fines and unlicensed-practice civil penalties rose from **$10,000 to $15,000** per violation. The qualifying-party and contract rules were also rewritten. An item written before 2025 would carry the old numbers. **Learn both. Current law is $15,000.** See the "how to reason" box in Section 5 of the guide.
