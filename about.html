Our Story

Roofing, Run Like an Operation.

Black Ridge Syndicate was formed around a simple idea: a roofing crew should operate with the same precision and accountability as any elite team. Every job — residential or commercial — gets the same process, the same documentation, and the same standard.

Today that means thousands of completed roofs, long-standing relationships with property managers, and a crew that homeowners refer to their neighbors.
