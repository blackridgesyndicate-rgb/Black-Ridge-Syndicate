Homeowner FAQ

Common Questions.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

We start every visit with an honest inspection. If a repair solves the problem, that's what we'll recommend — replacement only when it's genuinely the better long-term value.

Will you work directly with my insurance company?

Yes. We meet adjusters on-site, provide our own damage documentation, and review the claim scope to make sure it covers the actual cost of repair.

How long does a typical roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements are completed in one to two days, weather permitting, depending on the size and complexity of the roof.

Do you offer financing?

We can discuss financing options during your free estimate — ask your project consultant for current plans available in your area.
